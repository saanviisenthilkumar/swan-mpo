# Third-party notices

SWAN-MPO interoperates with or redistributes outputs from independently licensed/citable projects. Their authoritative terms control.

- **SwissADME** — physicochemical/ADME predictions. SwissADME's published Terms of Use designate Licensed Materials as CC BY 4.0 and require citation of Daina, Michielin & Zoete (2017). https://www.swissadme.ch/termsofuse.php
- **ProTox 3.0** — toxicity/liability predictions. The ProTox site links its Creative Commons notice to CC BY-ND 4.0. The repository includes only the exact unchanged project prediction export, with source attribution. https://tox.charite.de/protox3/
- **AutoDock Vina** — optional reference-ligand redocking executable.
- **RDKit** — audited upstream cheminformatics environment.
- **Open Babel** — audited upstream conversion/preparation environment.
- **NumPy** — numerical CLI dependency.
- **NetworkX** — graph-isomorphism dependency for symmetry-aware fixed-frame RMSD.
- **pytest** — development/test dependency.

No third-party receptor or ligand structure file is bundled.
